📐 FÓRMULAS DE REFERENCIA (recordatorio)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
BENEFICIO CONTABLE:
EBITDA = Net Income + Interests(net) + Taxes + D&A
= Ingresos - Gastos Explotación
= EBIT + D&A
EBIT = EBITDA - D&A
EBT = EBIT - Interests(net)
Net Income = EBT - Taxes
EPS = Net Income / Diluted Shares
VALORACIÓN:
Net Debt = ST Debt + LT Debt - Cash
Market Cap = Diluted Shares × Precio
EV = Market Cap + Net Debt
EV/EBITDA = EV / EBITDA
EV/EBIT = EV / EBIT
EV/FCF = EV / FCF
PER = Market Cap / Net Income = Precio / EPS
MÁRGENES %:
EBITDA Margin = EBITDA / Sales × 100
EBIT Margin = EBIT / Sales × 100
Net Margin = Net Income / Sales × 100
FCF Margin = FCF / Sales × 100
RENTABILIDAD %:
ROIC = EBIT × (1 - Tax Rate) / Invested Capital
Invested Capital = Equity + Deuda total + Op.Leases - Marketable Securities
ROE = Net Income / Equity
ROA = Net Income / Total Assets
FREE CASH FLOW:
WC = Inventarios + Cuentas Cobrar - Cuentas Pagar
ΔWC (CWC) = WC año actual - WC año anterior
· CWC POSITIVA → DISMINUYE FCF (financia aumento inventarios/cobros)
· CWC NEGATIVA → AUMENTA FCF (libera capital)
CapEx Mantenimiento ≈ D&A (atajo cuando CapEx total >> D&A)
🟢 FCF = EBITDA - Interests(net) - Taxes - CapEx Mant - ΔWC
